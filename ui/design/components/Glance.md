A floating preview of a link, opened with Alt+click, that can become a tab or a split.

**You provide:** the link's URL and the page it opens over.

- Size: 92% of the page width, 80% of its height, centred, on a 45% scrim. `radius-lg`, `shadow-glance`.
- The bar has the address, "Open as tab", "Split" and close. `Esc` or a click on the scrim closes it. Same space, same protection as the page beneath.
- Only one Glance at a time; opening a link inside it replaces its content.
- Don't use Glance for downloads or pop-up windows.
